The **DKU Computer Science Club** and **DKU AI Club** are preparing **Who Built TikTok? — Stories from a Decade of Mobile Internet**, a storytelling and career-sharing event planned for **Saturday, August 29, 2026**, at Duke Kunshan University.

The **DKU Computer Science Club** is a student-led community that brings students together through practical computing, interdisciplinary innovation, and flagship programs including HackDKU. Learn more on the [Club website](https://dkucs.com) and follow the [DKU Computer Science Club on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dkucompsciclub?trk=blended-typeahead).

The **DKU AI Club** is a diverse community of AI enthusiasts from all fields at DKU, working to make AI accessible, relevant, and exciting for everyone.

The clubs have invited **Cheng Li** and **Jingbo Huang** to join an accessible conversation about the people and teams behind TikTok, the development of the mobile internet over the past decade, and the career decisions that shape influential digital products.

## Event Information

- **Date:** Saturday, August 29, 2026
- **Location:** Duke Kunshan University
- **Time and venue:** To be announced
- **Organizers:** DKU Computer Science Club and DKU AI Club
- **Audience:** DKU students interested in technology, media, product development, business, interviews, and career development

No technical background will be required. The planned program will include short talks from both guests, a moderated panel discussion, and an open student Q&A.

## Planned Conversation

The discussion will explore:

- how the mobile internet industry has changed over the past decade;
- the people, teams, and decisions behind globally influential digital products;
- product development and leadership lessons from industry;
- interviews, internships, and early-career preparation;
- questions submitted directly by DKU students.

The final venue, speaker confirmation, and registration information will be announced when available.
